## BuildMaxHeap — Complexity

| Approach | Complexity |
|---|---|
| Repeated Insert (n × MaxHeapInsert) | O(n log n) |
| Bottom-up MaxHeapify (i = ⌊n/2⌋ down to 1) | O(n) |
